\begin{sourcescontribs}
The theory presented in this chapter is mostly covered in the first four subsections(14.4.1 - 14.4.4) of \parencite[Chapter 14.4, pp. 175-185]{foundations2}. In addition to the theory provided in this chapter, the authors also prove representation and uniqueness theorems for decomposability, interdimensional subtractivity and interdimensional additivity. Since some of these theorems require additional terminology, all three theorems have been omitted to focus on the AD Model.\\
All of the axioms presented here relate to axioms of the structures, that have been the base of the reduction. Decomposability, intradimensional subtractivity and interdimensional additivity utilize reduction to a decomposable conjoint structure, absolute difference structure and a additive conjoint measurement, respectively. These are presented in chapter 7, chapter 4.10 and chapter 6 in \parencite{foundations1}.
Other useful references include \parencite{foundationsMDS}, \parencite{tversky1970dimensional} and chapters 13, 15 and 16 in \parencite{TheorieDesMessens}.

\cref{def-p-exp-metric-family} is stated as an exercise in \parencite[p. 79, Exercise 19]{foundations2} and its proof is provided by the author of this thesis. \cref{rem-mink-pexp} has been outlined in \parencite[p. 73]{foundations2}.
