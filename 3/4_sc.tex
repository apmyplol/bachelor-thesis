The theory presented in this chapter is mostly covered in the first four subsections (14.4.1-14.4.4) of \textcite[Chapter 14.4, pp. 175-185]{foundations2}. In addition to the theory provided in this chapter, the authors also prove representation and uniqueness theorems for decomposability, (intradimensional) subtractivity and (interdimensional) additivity. Since some of these theorems require additional terminology, all three theorems have been omitted to focus on the AD Model.\\
Establishing decomposability, subtractivity and additivity relies on a reduction to a decomposable conjoint structure, absolute difference structure and an additive conjoint structure, respectively. These are presented in Chapter 7, Chapter 4.10 and Chapter 6 in \textcite{foundations1}. The corresponding axioms are closely related and are also presented in these chapters.
Other useful references include \textcite{foundationsMDS}, \textcite{tversky1970dimensional} and chapters 13, 15 and 16 in \textcite{TheorieDesMessens}.

\cref{def-p-exp-metric-family} is stated as an exercise in \textcite[p. 79, Exercise 19]{foundations2} and its proof is provided by the author of this thesis. \cref{rem-mink-pexp} has been outlined in \textcite[p. 73]{foundations2}.
